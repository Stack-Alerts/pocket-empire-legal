Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Overview

We collect essentially no personal information from viewers. Our content is viewed on third-party platforms (such as TikTok and YouTube), which have their own privacy practices that we do not control.

2. Information we collect

  • We do not require accounts and do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal data from people who watch our content.
  • If you contact us through the form, we collect the name, email address and message you provide, and use them only to reply.
  • The platforms that host our content may collect analytics and set cookies; please refer to their respective privacy policies.

3. Affiliate links and third parties

Our content may link to third-party merchants (for example Amazon and TikTok Shop). Those merchants may track purchases and set cookies under their own policies. We may receive a commission on qualifying purchases.

4. How we use information

We use information only to respond to inquiries and operate our channels. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data retention and security

Messages sent through the contact form are retained only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable records. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold.

6. Your rights

You may contact us to access or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you (which, in practice, is minimal).

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are reflected on this page.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact form.
